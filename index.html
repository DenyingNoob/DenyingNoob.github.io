Velith Smiths Diary. Reconstruction from found files

I found these files on hacker Velith Smith during a routine insepction of Obsidian Medicine's servers. I decided to upload/reconstruct the ones that I could for educational purposes. I have also translated them to English. I have created this site as if they were typed on a terminal from that time.

17/09/1992

Dear computer? I have found out how I can make a file on the internet now. I hope one day someone stumbles apon this and reads it.
For now I will just write what I can on this.

19/09/1992

Hello again. I found out my girlfriend fell out of love with me the other day. She says we should break it off before we get too far. But I don't really want to.
I love her a lot and leaving her feels wrong. But she is right. If we aren't in love then why would we stay, it only makes life harder to live in anyway.
I love you forever, Jackie. <3

25/09/1992

Its been a couple of days. Lets just say that I have had a lot of time to think about stuff. I have thought about what I want to be later in my life and well, I kinda have found myself at a crossroad. I have no one outside of the people here, no family, no love (maybe), no hometown that I can return to. I am almost utterly alone.
I always hope that things will change but with the way everything is going I doubt it. I am good with computers and the teachers tell me I can make a good teacher as well, but that doesn't really call me.
I always saw myself as more the person that will help older people with their technology, not teaching.
Anyway, I still have a lot of time. And this is a good way to train myself to work with all the technology as well. Not everyone can say that they have a diary on the internet.

27/02/1994

It's my birthday. Yay, I am 17 now. Around time next year I am out of here. I will miss it here a lot, but in the words of nun Roxanne. "You can't keep dwindeling on the past, dear. You gotta keep fighting and moving on. Even if it hurts and pains you. At the end of the road you will be happy you did it."
She is really old and really wise. Okay maybe not so old but she is really wise. If only she was my parent. I am sure I could have learned a lot of things from her. Well, I have enough time to be sad during the year. The boys are throwing me a birthday party. Hopefully the nuns won't catch us this time.

24/08/1995

I packed my bags and this will probably be the last entry for a while. I will be put on the street, against the will of the nuns, but I have to go. As much as I love this place and as much as I don't want to leave. I have to end this part of the story. Everyone is still at their parents/guardians places and at this point, I don't think I want them here. It will make it harder to leave.
I have made little gifts for all my friends and I hope I will live forth in their minds but this is statistically the end of our friendship. I will miss it here. And I will miss this computer. I hope the next person will find some time to read this and if not then the server I made this site on hopefully will live on.
Goodbye, and hopefully till another entry takes place.

News Article 15/03/2004

Hacker Velith Smith found dead

Velith Smith, known for being a hacker that helped a group of thieves steal thousands of Marks, has been found dead in a factory.
Her living family who she has had no contact with for the past 20 years says they had no idea she was even back in town.
The funeral was held in a small company where only family was allowed to be.
